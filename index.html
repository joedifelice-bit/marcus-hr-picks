How the model works

Probability: P(HR) = HR/PA vs pitcher hand × Park HR factor × Pitcher HR/9 multiplier (60% L5 + 40% season) × Form factor × Expected PA.

Fair odds: If P < 0.5 → +(1−P)/P × 100. EV %: P × FD payout − (1−P).

Tiers: BET = EV ≥ +5% & confidence ≥ B−. MONITOR = marginal (−5% to +5% EV) or +EV at lower confidence. PASS = clearly −EV at quoted FD line.

Anti-"due" logic: drought streaks are not boosters. Recent HR streaks only count when supported by sustained barrel/hard-hit data.
